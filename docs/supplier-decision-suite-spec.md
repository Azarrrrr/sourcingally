# Supplier Decision Suite: build specification

## Goal

Create four buyer-decision page families in English, Spanish, Portuguese, Russian, Turkish, and French. Every page must help a visitor make one specific next decision and must state what its evidence does and does not establish. The pages are designed to deepen the non-blog platform, not duplicate the existing services or Journal.

## Shared page structure

1. **Decision-led hero:** a clear buyer question and a short explanation of why it matters.
2. **When this is useful:** three project situations where the page matters.
3. **Evidence path:** four to five practical checkpoints or questions, expressed as a sequence rather than a generic list.
4. **What it does not prove:** visible limits that prevent a sample, a visit, a registration check, or an inspection from being presented as a guarantee.
5. **Prepare before acting:** a short, practical buyer-input checklist.
6. **Next routes:** three internal links to relevant service pages, tools, city content, Questions, or Journal articles.
7. **Project brief call-to-action:** a non-pushy link to start a structured sourcing conversation.

## Page families and link strategy

| URL slug | Buyer decision | Main internal paths |
|---|---|---|
| `supplier-verification` | Whether a supplier should progress to samples, a visit, a trial order, or further questions. | Supplier sourcing, Supplier verification Questions, factory check service, Journal guide. |
| `product-samples` | Whether a product version is ready to be approved for production or needs a revision. | Sample sourcing, Product Brief Builder, quality-control planning, sample Journal guide. |
| `factory-visits` | Whether an in-person factory visit is justified and what the buyer should capture. | Factory checks, China Route Map, Shenzhen/Guangzhou/Foshan/Dongguan content, Fees & scope. |
| `quality-control-plan` | What standards and checkpoints should be in place before production and shipment. | Quality control service, product-sample page, packaging service, quality-control Questions. |

## Evidence boundary

No page may claim that a supplier is “safe,” that an audit/visit/inspection guarantees future production, or that legal, customs, testing, or commercial outcomes are assured. Each page must distinguish observation, documents, product evidence, and the buyer’s final approval.

## Localization standard

All six locales should be natural and buyer-friendly, not literal translations. Each internal link must preserve the selected language prefix. Non-English pages must never expose English fallback body copy.

## Design standard

The new pages use the established bright editorial system: cobalt/electric blue, warm white, yellow and red accents, structured grids, ample content width, and responsive hover states. They should look like a decision tool or field guide, not a collection of link cards.
