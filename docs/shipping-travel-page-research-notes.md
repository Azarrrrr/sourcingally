# Shipping Readiness and China Business Travel: Research Notes

## Purpose

These notes support two client-facing educational page families. They should help buyers organize decisions and questions before shipping or visiting China. They must not give legal, customs, immigration, tax, or safety advice specific to a visitor, product, origin, or destination country.

## Shipping-readiness page: stable factual foundations

International Commercial Terms (Incoterms®) allocate specified delivery tasks, costs, and risks between buyer and seller. The Incoterms® 2020 rules comprise 11 terms and distinguish terms for any mode of transport from those for sea and inland-waterway transport. They do **not** determine payment timing, transfer of title, product conformity, all documentation requirements, or dispute resolution. The page should therefore tell buyers to agree the named place and chosen version in writing, then have their forwarder and importer-of-record verify the shipment-specific responsibilities.

Common documents can include a commercial invoice, packing list, transport document, and—in some cases—a certificate of origin or product- and destination-specific documents. Document content and import requirements vary by product, shipment, and destination. A forwarder and the importer should confirm the requirements before cargo release or collection.

## China business-travel page: stable factual foundations

A factory trip is most useful when meetings are pre-arranged, requirements are translated into questions, and travel time is organized around a limited number of decision-critical visits. A productive route should account for the factory location, realistic travel time, buyer role, agreed meeting goals, and how findings will be documented after each visit.

China entry, transit, hotel registration, payment access, local transport, telecommunications, and business etiquette can be relevant to a visit, but conditions change and depend on nationality, itinerary, visa or other entry basis, local accommodation, and location. The page should direct visitors to verify immigration and travel conditions with the relevant Chinese embassy or consulate, carrier, accommodation, and official sources before departure. It should not state eligibility or attempt to decide whether a particular traveller may enter.

Independent factory travel support is stated as **US$150 per day** when the client does not order through Sourcing Ally. This must be presented as an agreed service scope, not a guarantee of outcomes or approvals.
