Starting a sourcing project is easier when the product requirements are clear. Before contacting suppliers, prepare the information you already know: the product type, materials, dimensions, target quantity, packaging requirements, destination market, and target timeline.

## A useful first brief

A supplier or sourcing partner can work more efficiently when your first message includes the basics. If some details are not decided yet, say so. It is better to identify an open question than to hide it.

A practical brief usually includes the following:

- Product description and reference images.
- Desired materials, dimensions, colors, and functions.
- Expected order quantity and possible repeat orders.
- Packaging, labeling, and branding requirements.
- Destination country and target delivery date.

## Questions worth asking

You should understand whether a supplier is a factory, a trading company, or another type of business. You should also ask about minimum order quantities, sample availability, production timing, customization, and the information they can provide about quality checks.

There is no single supplier that is right for every project. The goal is to compare options against your actual requirements, not only against the lowest quoted price.
